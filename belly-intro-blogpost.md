# belly intro blog post

When my husband and I decided to take a leap and try our hands at building an
independent product studio from scratch, we knew it would be hard, but I don't
think we were ready for the rollercoaster ride that is bootstrapped
entreprenuership.

Our first product [Workbench](https://useworkbench.ca/)(which we still believe
in) grew to a stable amount of users. But after months of us trying everything
we could think of to get more businesses on the platform, we realized that we'd
hit a creative wall.

Belly was meant to be a fun little rest stop to get the creative juices flowing
again before jumping back into the world of b2b cold calls and in-person sales
visits.

We love cooking from home and all the positive financial and wellness benefits
that that has had in our lives; especially as early carreer kiddos living in
expensive Vancouver.

So the design direction was simple, how do I take this activity that we do
everyday (planning and making what we eat) with an aesthetic that makes me
smile.

After building and sharing the V1 of Belly to some close friends, we realized
something - we were having fun again!. We started IT'S US STUDIO because we love
making things and we love seeing the things we've made bring joy those who
engage with them.

Seeing friends share recipes with others in their lives, make meal plans, and
send us encouraging text messages about how much they've loved using Belly
reminded us of why we started our studio in the first place.

There's a lot for us to learn but my vision is to see Belly become the best tool
for anyone like me who wants to live and feel their best through calm and
inspiring meal planning.

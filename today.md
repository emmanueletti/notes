Week by week

- Week 1 (Nov 18–22):
  - Update your resume, list 15 target companies and contact 5 people from your
    Shopify network.
  - Set up a separate Greenhouse savings fund with automatic transfers.
  - Turn on disk encryption and backups.
  - Make the first commit of the repo.
  - Start Buy Then Build, write your first journal entry, and keep your sabbath.
- Week 2 (Nov 23–29):
  - Apply to 5 or more jobs.
  - Finish Buy Then Build and the Stanford primer.
  - Learn the P&L with the mentor agent.
  - Start French: 20–30 minutes a day plus a weekly class. Check whether
    Francisation Québec's free courses apply to you.
  - Book wills and powers of attorney. In Quebec, a will drawn up by a notary is
    common.
- Week 3 (Nov 30–Dec 6):
  - Interviews.
  - Financial Intelligence for Entrepreneurs, the balance sheet and cash flow,
    and your first quiz.
  - Get disability insurance quotes.
  - Sign off family firewall v1 with your wife, including how you'll cover the
    first-year salary gap.
  - Join Searchfunder.
- Week 4 (Dec 7–13):
  - Learn SDE versus EBITDA and add-backs.
  - Invite the $13M owner to coffee. Ask about their story and their mistakes.
    Save the board seat for later unless it comes up naturally.
  - Do your first monthly count of listings.
  - Ask the scout to dig deeper on commercial cleaning and on fire and
    life-safety inspection.
- Week 5 (Dec 14–18):
  - Push for a job offer, but don't take a bad one out of urgency.
  - Read the niche reports.
  - Evaluate one peer program within the $500/month budget.
  - Do a monthly review with your wife (time, energy, marriage, savings) and
    with the mentor agent.
  - Write your second journal entry.

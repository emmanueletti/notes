# indie coffee brand research

Montreal is a hub for third-wave indie coffee, with 13 active independent
roasters mapped across the city as of July 2026. The scene is concentrated in
neighborhoods like Mile-Ex, Saint-Henri, and Rosemont–La Petite-Patrie, with the
Canadian Roasting Society in Lachine serving as a key cooperative facility for
smaller brands.

Top independent roasters recognized for quality and innovation include:

Café Saint-Henri: Quebec’s first specialty micro-roaster, known for its
small-batch program and wide footprint. ZAB Café: The most-decorated roasting
program in Quebec, winning Th3rdwave awards multiple times. Binocle: Quebec’s
first 100% carbon-neutral roaster, using electric, ventless equipment and
bicycle delivery. Dispatch Coffee: Founded as a bike delivery service, now
operating a roastery-cafe in Mile-Ex with direct-trade sourcing. Paquebot: A
pioneer in nitro cold brew on tap, partnering closely with ZAB for its beans.
Café Pista: Features a Loring S15 Falcon roaster and a bright, clean specialty
profile. Le Brûloir: An Ahuntsic institution with direct relationships with
farmers in Honduras. Za & Klo Les Torréfactrices: A women-owned micro-roaster
focused on uplifting women and queer people in the industry. Other notable indie
brands include Café Jungle (local delivery-only), Narval Café Lab (online
subscription-focused), Canal Roasters, Café Humble Lion, and Escape Coffee
Roasters.

Five strategies came out of one session (go-to-market, product-led growth, supply store, machine automation, SarahBijoux). Two founders can't run them all. This decides what happens now, next and not this quarter.

## Metric for the quarter

New shops that make a real ticket within 7 days of signing up, per week. Baseline: 2 of 4 trials (2026-11-07 to 2026-11-16). Target by 2027-02-21: 3 of every 4.

Why this one: every channel, loop and feature multiplies whatever happens after sign-up. Until new shops get started on their own, more traffic only fills the leaky bucket.

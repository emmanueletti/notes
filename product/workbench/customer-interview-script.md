# Customer interview script

One 20-minute conversation that answers every "validate first" question in the Workbench notes. Goal: 5 shops by 2026-12-05 (current shops first: Willy, SarahBijoux, Fort Richmond, JEWELLERY CLINIC, C Creations), then every new shop at its setup call.

## Rules

- Ask about what they did, not what they would do. "Walk me through the last time" beats "would you use".
- Quote their words, with the date, into the shop's notes. One shop is a signal, not a verdict.
- Don't pitch. If they ask about a planned feature, note the question and move on.
- Skip any section that doesn't fit the shop; never run over 20 minutes.

## 1. How they found us (2 min)

- How did you first hear about Workbench?
- If online: what did you search for, or which AI assistant suggested it? What did you ask it?
- What else did you look at before choosing?

Feeds: `2026-11-21-plg-strategy.md` (keyword map, AI recommendations).

## 2. Intake and speed (4 min)

- Walk me through taking in a job on a busy day. Where does the time go?
- Has a line ever built up while you typed? What happened?
- What arrives with a job today: a paper form, an email, a message, nothing? (For wholesale: can we photograph 10 real store forms?)
- What has to be done while the customer is still at the counter?

Feeds: `2026-11-21-machine-automation-strategy.md` (photo intake, take it in now and finish later).

## 3. Their other tools (4 min)

- How do you take payments today, and with what (Square, Shopify POS, a terminal, The Edge)? What annoys you about it?
- Do you sell online? Where?
- Where do you type the same customer or order details twice?
- Do you use ChatGPT or Claude in the shop? For what?

Feeds: the Shopify and Square investigation, payments, the MCP server.

## 4. Getting customers (4 min)

- What does "more customers" mean for you: new people, repeat customers, bigger jobs, other stores sending work?
- Where do new customers come from today?
- Has a customer ever said they found you through Google, ChatGPT or another AI assistant?
- Do you ask for reviews? How?

Feeds: "Getting found, for our shops" in `2026-11-21-plg-strategy.md`, growth add-ons.

## 5. Supplies (2 min)

- What do you use for job bags, envelopes and labels? Where do you buy them, what do they cost, how often do you reorder?
- Would a QR code you scan instead of type change anything at your counter?

Feeds: `2026-11-21-supply-store-strategy.md`.

## 6. Custom work (2 min, independent brands only)

- How do you track a custom piece today, from first conversation to delivery?
- What takes the most time in it?

Feeds: `sarahbijoux.md`, custom projects v2.

## 7. Close (2 min)

- If Workbench disappeared tomorrow, what would you miss most?
- Who else should we talk to? (Referral ask: a free month for each shop that joins.)

## After each interview

Add a dated entry to `customer-interviews.md` in this folder: shop, date, who, quotes per section, and one line on what it changes. Update the note each section feeds when an answer changes a decision.

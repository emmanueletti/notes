## 1. Term (hard end date)
- This agreement runs for **one (1) month**, from [start date] to [end date].
- It **does not auto-renew.** It continues beyond the end date only if both parties sign a written renewal. Silence or continued work does not extend it.
- The intent is a trial: both sides decide at the end whether to continue, on what terms, or to stop — with no obligation to continue.

## 2. What the Studio provides
- Up to **[8–10] hours per week** of development work toward the Partner's product, totaling roughly **[32–40] hours** over the trial.
- "Hours" covers hands-on development only. It [does / does not] include meetings, async support, planning, or research — specify: [____].
- Unused hours [do / do not] roll over.
- Scope for the trial: [brief description of what will be built]. Anything outside this is a new request, agreed in writing, and may change the terms.

## 3. What the Partner provides
- The following specific, agreed growth/marketing actions during the trial: [e.g., X dedicated posts to their N-follower channel; Y email sends to their list of Z subscribers; named placements and dates].
- These are defined as **actions the Partner controls and commits to perform** — not a guaranteed outcome.

## 4. How "is it working?" is judged
- Each party is measured on the **inputs they control**: the Studio on hours delivered and features shipped; the Partner on the marketing actions actually performed.
- User-growth numbers are a **shared signal both parties review together** to decide whether to continue — they are **not** a breach trigger, since neither party fully controls them.
- At the end of the trial both parties review progress and decide, in writing, whether to renew.

## 5. Ownership & No Equity (core protections)
- **No equity, ever.** This agreement grants the Partner **no equity, shares, stock, options, membership interest, ownership, profit share, or revenue share** in IT'S US STUDIO, in any of its products, or in any of its intellectual property — neither now nor at any future time, and on no theory whatsoever. The same applies in reverse to the Studio regarding the Partner's business.
- **Sole and complete consideration.** The *only* thing each party receives in exchange for its performance is the other party's services as described in Sections 2 and 3. Nothing else is owed or implied.
- **Fairness acknowledgment and waiver.** Each party agrees the consideration above is **fair, adequate, and full**, and each **waives any claim** — including for unjust enrichment, quantum meruit, or "I gave more than I received" — that it was under-compensated or that the exchange was unequal.
- **No expectation of ownership.** The Partner confirms it has no expectation of acquiring any ownership or founder interest in the Studio or its products through this work.

## 6. Intellectual Property
- **The Partner's product:** Code and deliverables the Studio creates specifically for the Partner's product belong to the Partner, **except** the Studio's pre-existing and reusable tools, libraries, frameworks, and know-how ("Background IP"), which remain the Studio's property. The Studio grants the Partner a non-exclusive license to use its Background IP only as embedded in the delivered work.
- **The Studio's products:** All IT'S US STUDIO products and IP remain **solely and exclusively** owned by the Studio. Any ideas, feedback, or contributions the Partner makes toward the Studio's products are assigned to the Studio royalty-free, and the Partner waives any claim to them.
- **Third-party / open-source components:** [state who is responsible for license compliance].

## 7. Independent contractors — not partners
- The parties are **independent contractors.** This is **not** a partnership, joint venture, agency, or employment relationship.
- Neither party shares in the other's profits or losses, and neither may bind the other or incur obligations on the other's behalf.
- Neither party will publicly describe the other as a "partner," grant titles or company email, or co-mingle funds.

## 8. Confidentiality
- Each party will keep the other's non-public code, plans, data, and user information confidential, use it only for this engagement, and not disclose it. This obligation survives the end of the agreement.

## 9. Handoff at the end
- At the end of the trial (or any later end of the engagement), the Studio will hand over the work product built for the Partner: [repository access, documentation, credentials], over a transition window of [X days].
- This makes a clean handoff to the Partner's future technical hire straightforward and removes ambiguity about what transfers.

## 10. Mutual Release (sign at end of trial and at any renewal)
- At the end of the trial — and again at each renewal, if any — both parties sign a short statement confirming: *"As of this date, all obligations are current, the exchange to date has been fair, and neither party has any outstanding claim against the other arising from this engagement."*
- Each such signed release is a full and final settlement of all claims between the parties up to its date.

## 11. No guarantees; limitation of liability
- Neither party guarantees a business outcome: the Studio does not warrant the Partner's product will succeed, and the Partner does not warrant any specific user-growth result.
- Except for breaches of confidentiality or IP, **each party's total liability to the other is capped at the fair market value of the services exchanged during the trial**, and neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages.

## 12. Entire agreement; no reliance
- This document is the **complete and entire agreement** between the parties. It replaces all prior or contemporaneous discussions, promises, or understandings, written or oral. There are no side deals.
- Each party confirms it is **not relying** on any representation outside this document.
- Any change must be in writing and signed by both parties.

## 13. Dispute resolution and fees
- The parties will first attempt to resolve any dispute through good-faith [mediation / arbitration] in [location], before any court action.
- **Prevailing-party fees:** in any dispute arising from this agreement, the losing party pays the prevailing party's reasonable legal fees and costs.

## 14. Termination
- Either party may end this agreement at any time on **[7] days' written notice.**
- On termination, the Studio completes the handoff in Section 9; the Partner ceases marketing commitments going forward.
- Sections 5, 6, 8, 10, 11, 12, and 13 **survive** termination.

## 15. Governing law
- This agreement is governed by the laws of **[jurisdiction]**, and the parties submit to its courts (subject to Section 13).

# Meditations

The society in which I find myself born into is designed to perpetuate a specific structure in which 2% of the population (net worth > $1m USD) lives off of the economic output of the remainder.

The structure is made of up of 3 levels:

1. **The Free:** Those with the financial resources to incentivize the other players in the system to play their parts. The system ultimately exists to protect their material comforts for as many future generations as possible
2. **The Enforcers**: an equally small percentage of people (usually from The Free) who hold the positions of power where they work to set the rules, restrictions, incentives, and punishments in which those below are made to power the system for the benefits of those above
3. **The Slaves**: the remaning majority of people serving as the economic batteries keeping the system functioning.

The genius of the system is that hard power is rarely ever used to make the vast majority of the population live in service of those at the top.

The slaves are mostly free to do as they wish with the only three assets they are born with - time, creativity, and physical energy. But through education, distractions, and hacked psychological manipulation, the slaves are convinced to trade these assets in return for entertainment, pleasure, and the illusion of stability.

By using mostly soft as opposed to hard power, the slaves, even though they barely receive a significant portion of the fruits their labours bring about and are increasingly pressed for more output, actually fight to keep the system going.

The distractions, pleasures, entertainment, education, and hacked psychological manipulation not only mines the slaves for their highly valuable resource of time, creativity, and physical energy but also keeps these incredible abilities in check.

These instruments of soft power makes sure that the slaves are kept for transcending to higher stations in life. Their ability to think critically, focus on hard problems, take financial risk, and even live healthy lifes without help from the pharmacenutical or medical industries are reduced since these abilities mean they are able to create value for themselves and thus leave less people to perpetuate the system for the Free.

In order to escape the system, moving from slave to free, one must use their core assets to build the financial resources that allows them to live without needing to trade their time and energy for money. They must have the personal discipline to ignore the siren call of consumerism (the stripping away of whatever savings a slave has in order to keep them a slave). The discipline to protect their health (the stripping away of the slaves independence from expensive medications and medical intervensions requiring benefits - staying employed or raiding savings via poor food, lack of exercise, poor air quality, high stress environments). The discipline to protect their focus (the stripping away of a slaves ability to solve problems or even realize their enslavement via entertainment and social media products)

If an enterprising slave begins to see that they are being taken advantage of and wishes to use their assets for themselves they are actively challenged through methods such as:

- an education system that gatekeeps employment by requiring you to first shackle yourself to significant debt 
- a consumer culture that asks you spend as much as possible on products and experienced pitched as 'must haves'
- judeo christian beliefs that make unquestioned labour a virtue
- judeo christian beleifs that make unquestioned family growth a virtue (having kids is Godly behaviour - not having kids is selfish)
- entertainment products that hack their physcology to make it difficult for them to think, focus
- entertainment products that require hours of their valuable time
- daily commutes that take hours and leave the mind exhausted

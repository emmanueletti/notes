ask bella

Eight takes, all lowercase: four dark, then the same four in oat. No gradients, glow or animation: matte colour, careful type, and one detail each. Each sits on a phone mock of the real page, with a close-up underneath.

Shimmer border on A

A1

Slow loop

A champagne glint travels around the 1px edge every 4 seconds, without stopping.

A2

Glint, then rest

One quick pass around the edge, then still for about 5 seconds. Catches the eye without fidgeting.

A3

Still foil

No motion: a fixed champagne-to-espresso edge, like foil catching light. The calmest version.

All options

A

Espresso, sans

Warm near-black, a tiny still champagne star, and "ask bella" in the app's sans with open letter spacing. Closest to a skincare label.

B

Espresso, serif

The same espresso with "ask bella" in italic Playfair and a tiny still champagne star. Softer and more personal.

C

Aubergine, ruled

Deep aubergine, italic serif, and a hairline champagne rule just inside the edge, like a debossed card.

D

Ink, monogram

The app's ink with a small blush disc holding an italic "b", then "ask bella" in spaced sans. The disc can become Bella's mark elsewhere.

E

Oat, sans

Oat with ink type and a hairline edge. The light twin of A: the quietest option on the page.

F

Oat, serif

Oat with italic serif and a small clay star. Warm, like linen stationery.

G

Oat, ruled

Oat with italic serif and a clay hairline just inside the edge. The light twin of C.

H

Oat, monogram

Oat pill with an ink disc holding the italic "b". The mark flips dark, so it still reads as Bella's.

These are mockups, not the app. Whichever you pick, the button shows on the prep plan only and opens the same Bella sheet, which can carry the same type so it feels like one piece.

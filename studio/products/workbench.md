## What it is

**Workflow-first job management for jewelry and watch shops handling repairs and custom work.**

Vertical SaaS. Replaces spreadsheets, paper binders, and generic project management tools with a purpose-built workflow system for shops that work on high-value, often irreplaceable items.

- **URL:** https://useworkbench.ca
- **App:** https://app.useworkbench.ca
- **Status:** Active. Live with paying customers.

## Who it serves

- **Primary:** Jewelry and watch repair shop owners and managers.
- **Secondary:** Jewelry and watch retailers with in-house repair services and custom project offerings.
- **Geography:** Canada (primary), United States (secondary).
- **Languages:** English and French (bilingual product).

## The problem

Jewelry and watch shops manage high-value items through complex multi-step workflows covering both repairs and custom project work. Most shops track jobs manually through paper binders, spreadsheets, or generic ticketing tools not designed for this kind of work. The result:

- Lost job information
- No photo documentation of piece condition (liability when customers dispute damage)
- No systematic customer communication (constant "is it ready?" calls)
- Job info scattered across Shopify, Google Sheets, WhatsApp, paper
- No searchable history (digging through physical archives)
- No team visibility (technicians don't know what others are working on)
- No signed intake agreements (verbal-only terms create disputes)

## Current feature set

- **Intake photos** — timestamped, attached permanently. Damage-dispute protection.
- **Customer notifications** — automatic SMS and email on status change. Bilingual EN/FR.
- **Team management** — assign jobs, internal notes, printable physical job tickets.
- **Customer CRM** — permanent profiles, full repair history, contact details across visits.
- **Workflow dashboard** — single-screen view, overdue flagging, browser-based.
- **Signed intake forms** — digital terms of service signed before work begins.

## The incumbents

These are the companies Workbench is taking customers from (or has to displace from spreadsheets / paper).

### The Edge (Abbott Jewelry Systems)
- Trusted, long-standing retail jewelry POS with a service-tracking module.
- Used by thousands of jewelry retailers.
- $4,600 USD upfront + 20% annual support, licensed per workstation.
- **Workbench wedge:** web-based, workflow-first, flat monthly CAD rate, no per-seat fees, focused on the bench rather than full retail. Best fit when shop already has a POS and wants a dedicated workflow tool.

### RepairShopr
- Well-established, feature-rich, loyal base of phone/computer/electronics shops.
- **Workbench wedge:** newer, fewer integrations, but jewelry/watch-tuned with bilingual notifications, CAD pricing, intake workflows around pieces, metals, stones, custom project descriptions.

### RepairTrax
- Long-standing, inexpensive, dependable for many kinds of repairs. POS, credit card, parts inventory, QuickBooks built-in.
- **Workbench wedge:** lean specialist for jewelry/watch, CAD pricing, bilingual EN/FR. **Strongest single difference: clean modern UI vs. RepairTrax's older, text-heavy, utilitarian style.** Modern SaaS feel, short learning curve.

### RepairDesk
- Modern, polished, broad feature set (POS, inventory, QuickBooks, 40+ integrations). Cellphone/computer/electronics primary positioning. From $99 USD/store/month.
- **Workbench wedge:** smaller, focused jewelry/watch UI, ~1/3 the price, bilingual EN/FR. Closing feature gap deliberately while protecting clarity.

### Spreadsheets / paper / generic tools (Asana, Trello, kanban)
- The default state for many shops.
- **Workbench wedge:** photos, notifications, signed terms, customer CRM, repair-job-as-core-object — none of which generic tools provide without heavy configuration.

## Differentiators (the wedge stack)

In rough order of strength:

1. **Workflow-first product design.** Job is the core object, covering repairs and custom work — not a service module on top of a retail POS.
2. **Purpose-built for jewelry and watch shops.** Not a generic tool with custom fields.
3. **Modern, clean UI.** Familiar to Shopify-era users. Short learning curve. Big gap vs. RepairTrax/older incumbents.
4. **Bilingual EN/FR notifications.** Critical for Canada.
5. **CAD pricing.** Cost advantage for US shops, FX stability for Canadian shops.
6. **Flat-rate pricing.** Unlimited team and jobs. No per-seat or per-workstation friction.
7. **Bootstrapped + independent.** Product decisions made in customer interest, not investor interest.
8. **Small team, fast iteration.** Feature requests get implemented. Support is fast.
9. **No install, fully browser-based.** Works on any device.

## Not a fit

- Pure retail or e-commerce businesses with no in-house bench services.
- Large enterprise or multi-national repair chains requiring complex ERP integration.
- Repair shops outside jewelry/watch (electronics, automotive, etc.).
- Businesses requiring native integrations with specific POS/accounting at launch.

## Claims to make

- Workflow-first job management built around the job as the core object.
- Purpose-built for jewelry and watch shops.
- Supports both repair and custom project work (not repair-only).
- Automatic bilingual EN/FR customer notifications.
- Flat-rate pricing, no per-seat fees.
- 30-day free trial, full feature access.
- Bootstrapped Canadian company.

## Claims to avoid

- Do not describe Workbench as repair-only — it supports custom jobs and project work.
- Do not describe Workbench as a POS — no payment processing or retail inventory.
- Do not describe Workbench as inventory management — it manages jobs, not inventory.
- Do not describe it as a service module or add-on — the job workflow is the core product.

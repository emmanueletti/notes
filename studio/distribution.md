# Distribution

## The honest role of outbound

Outbound is not optional for what we are building. "Niche overlooked by incumbent" means **the audience already exists, is identifiable, and can be reached directly.** That is an outbound dream, and not using it is leaving the easiest leverage on the table.

Inbound and brand are the long game. Outbound is what gets us our first 100 paying users and — more importantly — our first 100 user conversations. We cannot build a loved product for users we have never talked to.

## Outbound by stage

**Stage 0 — pre-product (now → first 10 users):**
Pure founder-led outreach. Not selling — *learning*. 5-10 personalized messages a day to people in the niche. Goal is conversations, not conversions. Every "no" teaches us what we are missing. This is the most valuable work we can do, and AI cannot do it for us.

**Stage 1 — early product (10 → 100 users):**
Founder-led outbound is still the engine. Now we are converting, but the message is "we built this with people like you, want to try it?" Personalized, warm, slow. 10-20 thoughtful touches a day beats 500 cold emails.

**Stage 2 — proven product (100 → 1000 users):**
Outbound starts giving way to inbound + community + word of mouth. We stop scaling outbound — we scale the *brand* that makes outbound unnecessary. But we never stop doing some, because it keeps us close to the market.

**Stage 3 — portfolio:**
Each new product launches with the audience the previous one built. The portfolio brand becomes the outbound engine. This is the long-term unlock — and why year 1-2 of brand investment matters even when it feels slow.

## What good outbound looks like

- **Personalized, not templated.** If AI wrote it without our edits, do not send it.
- **Useful before asking.** Lead with insight, observation, or a tool, not a pitch.
- **One channel, deeply.** Pick where the niche actually lives (Twitter, LinkedIn, niche forum, Discord, email) and dominate one before adding another.
- **Founder-signed.** "Hey, I'm one of two people building this" outperforms any sales title at this scale. Do not hide behind a brand voice.
- **Track replies, not sends.** The metric is conversations started, not messages delivered.

## Weekly outbound cadence

- **Daily: 30-60 min outbound block.** Same time every day. 10-20 personalized touches. Non-negotiable for the first 12-18 months.
- **Daily: respond to every reply within 24 hrs.** Speed of response is a competitive advantage indies have over incumbents. Do not waste it.
- **Weekly: review outbound metrics.** Reply rate, conversation rate, conversion rate. Kill what is not working, double what is.

## Brand-as-distribution

The portfolio brand is the long-term distribution asset. Investments that compound:

- **A public voice.** One of us writes regularly under our own name. About the work, the niches, what we are learning. Not corporate. Honest, opinionated, distinctive.
- **Build-in-public artifacts.** Demos, teardowns, postmortems, behind-the-scenes. People follow process, not products.
- **Studio identity.** Same visual language, voice, point of view across products. The "made by us" mark should mean something specific.
- **Audience capture.** Email list, owned channel. Not just product users — *audience.* People who follow the studio, not just the tool.
- **Slow trust.** A reputation for shipping things that work, telling the truth about what does not, and not selling out. This takes years and cannot be faked.

## What to avoid

- **Outsourcing outbound early.** SDRs, agencies, automated sequences. All of it. We are not buying pipeline, we are buying market understanding — and that does not outsource.
- **Cold-email tools that promise scale.** Spray-and-pray will burn our domain reputation and our brand simultaneously. Both are hard to recover.
- **Treating outbound as separate from product.** Every outbound conversation should feed product decisions. If our outbound and product threads do not connect, we are doing one of them wrong.
- **Paid acquisition at indie scale.** Almost always a leak, not a channel. Earn the audience.
- **Launching loud, then going dark.** Worse than no launch. Cadence matters more than peaks.

## The reframe

Outbound is not the un-loved cousin of "build great product." For a niche-attacker, **outbound IS product research with a conversion rate attached.** We are not interrupting our real work to do sales — sales calls are our real work, especially in year one.

Slot it in. Daily. Forever, in some form.

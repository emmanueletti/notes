# Channel choice: in-person primary

**Date:** 2026-04-17
**Reversibility:** Partially reversible (channels can be re-tested if data changes)

## Context

Workbench has 10 paying customers. We've been running both digital outreach (cold email, LinkedIn) and in-person store visits. The data after limited side-project effort:

- **Digital outreach:** No conversions. Some replies, no real conversations leading to signups.
- **In-person store visits:** 10 visits → 1 paid customer. Plus the most substantive conversations and useful objections we've gathered.

## Options considered

1. **Continue mixing digital + in-person.** Keep optimizing both, hoping digital improves.
2. **Pivot to in-person primary, kill digital outreach.** Commit fully to the channel showing signal.
3. **Pivot to community / trade-show primary.** Skip the cold-visit grind, go where buyers gather.

## Decision

**Option 2.** In-person store visits become the primary channel. Digital outreach is parked.

Trade-show + trade-publication presence will be added in Phase 2 as concentration plays of the same in-person logic.

## Why

- The market signal is consistent: jewelry/watch shop owners are physical-store craftspeople, often older, who buy from people they have met in person. Trust is the limiting factor when asking them to put their entire workflow into a software vendor.
- Digital outreach is not just underperforming — it's producing zero signal in this niche. Continuing to optimize a dead channel is the indie-founder failure mode.
- The principle in `~/notes/studio/distribution.md` says "one channel, deeply" before adding another. We pick the one with signal.
- The cost of being wrong (committing to in-person) is bounded: if it stops converting after 100+ visits, we re-evaluate.

## What this means we accept

- The "AI multiplies headcount" operating model bends for this product. In-person sales is the one function AI cannot multiply. We accept this consciously — see `path-b-bootstrap-then-mouth.md` for the full reasoning.
- Founder time goes into visits, not into building features or running automated funnels.
- The pace is bounded by founder hours until word-of-mouth takes over.

## Revisit trigger

Revisit this decision if:

- We hit 100+ in-person visits and conversion has dropped meaningfully below 10%.
- A digital channel suddenly starts producing signal (unlikely but worth watching).
- A partner channel emerges that effectively scales in-person without our hours.
- Word-of-mouth begins producing inbound at meaningful volume (this is the natural sunset for in-person being primary).

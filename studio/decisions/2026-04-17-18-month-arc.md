# 18-month strategic arc to 150-200 customers

**Date:** 2026-04-17
**Reversibility:** Reversible at each phase boundary

## Context

We previously framed the milestone as "125 customers for ramen profitable." Updated context (FIRE-funded ~10-year runway, ~$3K/mo total burn) makes "ramen profitable" less load-bearing as a milestone — we are not chasing survival.

We need a strategic arc that is honest about the channel reality (in-person), the runway (long), and the failure mode (drift).

## Options considered

1. **9-month sprint to 125.** Work hard, hit ramen-profitable, then breathe.
2. **18-month arc to 150-200.** Pace it correctly, build the case studies and trade reputation that enable Phase 3 (word-of-mouth carrying load).
3. **24+ month patient arc to 200+.** Even slower, optimize for product depth and brand more than customer count.
4. **No fixed arc.** Just keep visiting and see what happens.

## Decision

**Option 2.** 18-month strategic arc, three phases:

| Phase | Window | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Phase 1: Find the rhythm | Months 1-3 (Apr-Jul 2026) | 25-35 customers |
| Phase 2: Saturate | Months 4-9 (Aug 2026-Jan 2027) | 75 customers |
| Phase 3: Compound | Months 10-18 (Feb-Oct 2027) | 150-200 customers |

Each phase has a milestone date with an internal consequence if missed (full-day off-site, honest diagnosis, decide to continue or change). See `~/notes/studio/finances.md` for the consequence framework.

## Why

- Option 1 (9-month sprint) is fast enough to risk burnout and shallow enough to not build the case studies, trade reputation, and word-of-mouth needed for Phase 3.
- Option 3 (24+ month) is too slow given the runway-induced drift risk.
- Option 4 (no fixed arc) is the path most likely to end in "month 30 arrived, $250K spent, 30 customers." We will not accept that.
- 18 months matches the natural shape: 3 months to find rhythm, 6 months to saturate metros + start trade-press, 9 months for word-of-mouth to begin compounding.

## What this means we accept

- Working on a 6-week-cycle cadence (Shape Up) inside the 18-month arc. Quarterly direction-setting checks the arc itself.
- Each missed milestone triggers a consequence we have committed to in advance. This is the discipline that prevents drift.
- "150-200" is a realistic range, not a hard number. The deeper success measure is whether word-of-mouth has begun compounding meaningfully.

## Revisit trigger

Revisit this arc at each phase boundary (Day 90, Month 9). Always at the quarterly direction-setting offsite.

Specifically, abandon or rewrite the arc if:

- Phase 1 misses badly (under 15 customers at Day 90) — channel itself may be wrong, or we may be wrong about full-time productivity.
- Phase 2 hits 75 customers ahead of schedule with strong inbound momentum — accelerate Phase 3.
- New evidence emerges that the niche is much smaller than we believed (TAM too tight to support 200 paying customers). → Pivot product or accept smaller end state per Path (c) in the path-b decision file.

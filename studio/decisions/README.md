# Decisions Log

Each significant decision gets its own file. Filename: `YYYY-MM-DD-short-slug.md`.

## Format

Every decision file should have:

- **Date** — when the decision was made
- **Context** — what was happening, what triggered the decision
- **Options considered** — at least the alternatives that were live
- **Decision** — what we chose
- **Why** — the reasoning, including which principle or evidence drove it
- **Reversibility** — reversible / irreversible / partially reversible
- **Revisit trigger** — what would cause us to revisit this decision

## When to log

Log a decision when:

- The choice will shape the next 30+ days of work
- Future-us would want to know the reasoning ("why did we do that?")
- The decision conflicts with or interprets a north-star principle
- The decision involves money, time commitment, or a significant external commitment
- The decision parks an alternative we want to remember

Do not log every small choice. Log the ones that matter.

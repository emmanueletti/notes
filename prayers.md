# Prayers

I am loved and forgiven.
Given strength to choose the narrow way that leads to joy.
Given all of the good things of life,
An amazing spouse (who I take for granted)
Good health
A joyful craft 
Many friends
And much much more
My sinful self and the voice that lies,
will try to tell me its all not enough
That God is not good, nor is he for me
That the world has much better gifts to give
That I am not forgiven.
I must not listen to these lies but instead ask for strength
To take the narrow and simple way that leads to joy.

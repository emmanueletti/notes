Mackenzie

## Dos

- Spend two minutes on their site first. Get the exact program name and note
  anything about meal plans, recipes or grocery lists.
- Check their booking or portal link. practicebetter.io, trainerize.com,
  everfit.io or truecoach.co tells you what they already pay for.
- Send on whichever channel they are more active on, email or Instagram.
- Open with something specific to them. Keep DMs under about 70 words and emails
  under 120.
- Say the findings are aggregated and anonymised. In a DM, say it in the second
  message.
- Answer confidentiality questions plainly if they ask first.
- Send links only after they reply.
- Keep the sender name and the no thanks line in every email. Add a mailing
  address if the sending tool supports it.
- Keep daily volume human: 15 to 20 emails per address, fewer DMs on a new
  Instagram account.

## Don'ts

- Don't pitch, mention pricing, or ask if they would buy something.
- Don't attach a deck or demo, or link to the app in the first message.
- Don't say "quick call" or "pick your brain".
- Don't offer a free account as the incentive.
- Don't name a coach to another coach or share anything identifiable, even when
  asked on a call.
- Don't personalise with something you did not actually read.
- Don't send more than one follow-up.
